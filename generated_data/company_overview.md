# Brightwater Packaging Systems, Inc.

## Company name and one-line description
**Brightwater Packaging Systems, Inc.** ("Brightwater" or "BPS") is a custom packaging-machinery OEM that designs and builds engineer-to-order filling, capping, case-packing, and end-of-line machines for food, beverage, CPG, and pharmaceutical plants, along with spare parts and service contracts.

- **Email domain:** brightwaterpkg.com
- **Industry:** Industrial machinery / packaging automation

## Mission and vision
**Mission:** Help manufacturers run flexible, reliable packaging lines by delivering custom automation systems backed by responsive aftermarket support.

**Vision:** Be the preferred mid-market packaging automation partner in North America for plants that need high-mix changeover flexibility, dependable execution, and long-term service support.

## Company overview
Brightwater Packaging Systems, Inc. is a privately held packaging automation company serving primarily North American food, beverage, CPG, and pharmaceutical manufacturers. The company designs, engineers, builds, tests, installs, and services custom packaging machines and retrofit solutions tailored to individual customer line requirements.

Brightwater operates as an engineer-to-order OEM. Most projects begin with an RFQ and application review, followed by quotation, revisions, purchase order, detailed engineering, fabrication, assembly, factory acceptance testing, installation, and commissioning. In addition to new machine builds, the company generates recurring business through spare parts, field service, preventive maintenance, remote support, and upgrade programs.

The business is positioned as a flexible mid-market OEM with strong aftermarket support rather than a low-cost commodity supplier.

## How the core product and technology work
Brightwater’s operating model combines applications engineering, custom machine design, controls development, in-house build, testing, and field deployment.

### Typical project flow
1. **Scoping and applications engineering**
   - Application engineers review customer requirements, package formats, throughput targets, plant constraints, and integration needs.
   - The team develops concept layouts, system assumptions, and commercial proposals.

2. **Detailed engineering**
   - Mechanical engineering develops the machine design and layout.
   - Controls engineering develops the PLC-based control architecture, HMI logic, motion control, and system integration.
   - Designs typically incorporate servo drives, motors, sensors, and pneumatics.

3. **Procurement and build**
   - Purchasing sources components and fabricated parts through an approximately 120-supplier network.
   - Systems are built and assembled in **Dayton, Ohio** or **Queretaro, Mexico** depending on capacity, cost, and program needs.

4. **Testing**
   - Machines undergo factory acceptance testing (FAT) before shipment.
   - FAT verifies functionality, motion sequences, safety systems, and agreed performance criteria.

5. **Installation and commissioning**
   - Field service teams install and commission equipment at customer sites.
   - After handoff, Brightwater supports startup stabilization, maintenance, troubleshooting, and future upgrades.

- Late supplier deliveries
- Engineering change orders (ECOs) during active builds
